How to use these

Before the lesson

Open the studio for the topic we are starting and play with the figures for five minutes. You are not expected to know the vocabulary yet — just notice what stays true when you drag things.

While you study

Use Solve with reasons and the Proof builder to check yourself. If a reason is not accepted, open the Fact bank and find the exact wording of the fact you need.

If something breaks

Refresh the page first. If a studio still will not load, message me on Classroom and tell me which one and what device you were on.
